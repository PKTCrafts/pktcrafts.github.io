Crafting for Hope

A creative art challenge and fundraiser for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital (2024–2025)

Crafting for Hope was a two-year creative initiative and community fundraiser dedicated to supporting children battling cancer. Across two chapters, artists and makers from around the world united to turn creativity into impactful hope.

This cause is close to our hearts. In 2024, our nine-year-old son Henry was diagnosed with a malignant brain tumor, turning our lives upside down overnight. After a long and heavy journey involving surgery, a year of chemotherapy, proton radiation, and time away from work and school, his outlook is now bright.

When everything felt uncertain, the incredible support from the crafting community and organizations like St. Jude represented what hope looks like. For families facing their worst days, hope is everything.

Together, our global crafting community raised $6,190 for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, ensuring that families never receive a bill for treatment, travel, housing, or food. Thank you for being a part of this journey and making a real-world difference.

Crafting for Hope

2024 & 2025 — Two years of creativity and community supporting children with cancer through St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital.

Over two years, talented artists and makers from around the world came together to create beautiful miniature art in support of St. Jude. Families at St. Jude never receive a bill for treatment, travel, housing, or food.
